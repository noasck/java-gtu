    WELCOME, ON-CALL ARCHITECT

    Seven services are about to hit production. You are the database: choose how requests move, how data is split, how many copies exist, and what the system does when the network tears in half.

    1 · READ THE SLACorrectness, availability, durability and budget change every level.
    2 · BUILDPick CAP behavior, replicas, shards and a request path.
    3 · SURVIVEWatch users, failures, queues, stale reads and infrastructure cost.

    Important: “P” is not a feature you can switch off. On partitioned levels, your real CAP choice is whether consistency or availability wins. A cheap design can collapse; an oversized one can fail the budget.

    FIELD MANUAL

    CA is useful shorthand for a non-partitioned/single-node scope: consistent and available while that scope works. It is not a magic answer to a network partition.

    CP preserves a single, current truth during a partition. A side without quorum must wait or reject work. Great for money, uniqueness and invariants.

    AP lets reachable nodes respond during a partition. Replicas may temporarily disagree and must reconcile later. Great when continued service beats immediate agreement.

    Shards split different data/work across nodes, increasing capacity. Replicas copy the same shard, improving failure tolerance and often reads, but costing money and write coordination.

    Direct is request/response. Pub/sub fans live messages to interested subscribers but this game's ephemeral broker cannot replay missed events. Stream retains events for acknowledgements, catch-up and replay. Sharded pub/sub hashes channels to owning broker shards to reduce cluster-wide fan-out.
